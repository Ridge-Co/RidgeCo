Tick the dates you want to change, then use the buttons at the bottom.
  • Swap cleaner: the cleaner you pick takes the ticked dates. Whoever had them is told they are off the hook and becomes backup. The cleaning calendar is fixed too.
  • Assign a range: one cleaner gets every turnover between two dates you choose.
  • Mark out / backup: sets a cleaner as Out or Available-as-backup for the ticked dates, or clears it.
