🧾 RIDGE CO — RECEIPT RECONCILER

Every receipt dropped in the Receipts and Invoices Drive folder gets OCR'd once, matched against your open work orders by description, and checked for duplicates — all with zero-AI pure code (CAP-002). Nothing bills itself. Confirm posts the receipt to the work order exactly like a manual entry; Skip dismisses it. If a receipt is flagged as an already-billed duplicate, use Confirm duplicate instead of Skip — that's a distinct, tracked resolution that auto-clears itself after 180 days instead of sitting in Skipped forever. The daily sweep runs on its own; use "Scan now" to pull in anything dropped since the last run.
