🔓 Tenant Work Order Access

How this works: tenants cannot submit their own work orders (via the online request link) unless you turn it on somewhere. You can turn it on/off two ways — an owner-level setting (which can apply to all of that owner's properties, or just ones you pick) and a property-level setting (which can apply to the whole property, or just units you pick). Owner-level always wins when it covers the property — set it there and it overrides whatever the property says. Nothing set anywhere = tenants can't submit (off by default).
