# STR pre-arrival auto-cancel fix + cancel-hold safeguard — 2026-11-09 — PR into main, head `fix/str-rollover-cancel-hold` (cut from main tip 99061aa2), merged: yes (Brett said "Merge it", Nov 9 08:49 ET)
## CURRENT
**Incident (Mon Nov 9, ~05:16Z):** the Nov 11 pre-arrival clean (`milam-ridge|prearrival|2026-11-12`, Gina) was auto-cancelled and Gina was texted "cancelled" although the Nov 12-15 booking (Alyssa Saula) is still live on both the Uplisting and Airbnb iCal feeds.
**Root cause:** `strBuildTasks` derived a pre-arrival task from the PREVIOUS stay's checkout date, and `strReadFeed`/`strBuildTasks` dropped stays with `end < today`. When the Nov 8 checkout rolled into the past at midnight Nov 9, the previous stay vanished, the pre-arrival task vanished, Missing_Count hit 2 on two ticks minutes apart, and the row was cancelled. Any pre-arrival clean whose previous checkout passes while the next guest is still 2+ days away would do this.
**Fix (worker.js STR block):**
1. Look-back window `strPreLookback(S)` = max(14, preGap+2) days for stays; pre-arrival tasks for future arrivals are generated even when the previous stay is outside the window; checkout tasks only emitted for date >= today.
2. 3-hour persistence grace (`STR_CANCEL_GRACE_MS`): a row must stay missing 3h after its last update before it can be cancelled (manual `pending_converted` rows keep the old behaviour).
3. HOLD instead of cancel: if a covered/ext_covered turnover (cleaner assigned) disappears, Note = `CANCEL HELD - waiting for Brett: <why>`, nothing is cancelled, the cleaner is NOT texted, the calendar entry stays, Brett gets a high-severity alert + SMS. Answer via `POST /str/admin/cancel-decision {task_key, action:'keep'|'cancel', notify?}`. `GET /str/admin/state` lists `cancel_holds`. If the booking comes back the hold clears itself.
4. Auto-restore: a cancelled row with an assigned cleaner whose note says "no longer on the feed" is restored to covered when its task reappears; the cleaner gets `str_task_restored` ("back on") and Brett gets a "Restored" alert. This is what repairs Nov 11 after deploy.
