# Premier payment-email watcher (Nov 5 2026) — PR into main, head `feat/premier-payment-watch`, merged: no
## CURRENT
**Why (Brett, Nov 3 evening):** "create an apps script watcher to check for payment emails. i don't know where they will come from. appfolio, premier etc. but they should say something like invoice paid etc. check once daily m-f until we see what they look like. check with me after 30 days if we haven't received anything."
**Finding that shaped it:** Brett's inbox already holds ONE real AppFolio vendor-payment email (Aug 18 2026, "Your Vendor Payments as of ...", from donotreply@appfolio.com to brett@, body "Initiated: $X Vendor Payment to you", Payment Overview with Total / Confirmation Number / Date, "Payment #N Details" with Amount / Property Name/Address / Description). It was a referral payment, not an inspection, so we still do not know how an inspection payment names its invoice or WO. Premier pays through AppFolio ACH, and the mail lands in brett@ (the same Google account the scheduler runs as).
**Built:** a new row `premier_payment_watch` in the scheduler JOBS table (`apps-script/hub-scheduler/HubScheduler.gs`), `run: 'gmail_watch'`, Mon-Fri 07:45 ET. New schedule style `weekdays: true` for non-Hub rows (validated; due/overdue/seed logic skip Sat/Sun). The runner (section 5b) does three Gmail searches since the last run (36 h overlap, de-duplicated by message id): (1) the exact AppFolio "Vendor Payments" shape, (2) anything from AppFolio / Premier senders that mentions payment words, (3) subject phrases only: invoice paid / paid invoice / remittance / ACH payment / payment advice. Tried on Brett's real inbox: (1) 1 hit, (2) 4 hits of which 3 are known housekeeping (filtered), (3) 1 hit in a year. Two wider versions of (3) matched 74 and 684 emails a year of noise and were rejected.
**What it does:** READS AND REPORTS ONLY. Emails Brett (a) once at start ("Payment watcher is ON", also lists anything from the last 7 days), (b) whenever new payment-looking emails appear (amount, confirmation number, payment lines, any WO / invoice numbers mentioned, a Gmail link), (c) when 30 days pass with none (then again every 30 days while still none). A quiet day sends nothing, but the job still reports ok to the scheduler (statusReport shows `Payment watcher: ...`). Known non-payment AppFolio mail (Online Payment Confirmation, Payment Method Updated, 2-Step, Request to Access, FolioSpace, Documents Request) and other businesses' receipts (Klarna, Square, T-Mobile, dume.ai) are counted and skipped, never emailed.
**Safety:** no HTTP call, no Hub/QuickBooks call, no Gmail label/send/archive/trash (a test enforces both). AppFolio emails carry a magic link that logs into the portal without a password: only labelled fields are copied, never a link or the raw body (test uses a fake token and asserts it never appears in the email or logs). State (`PAYMENT_WATCH` script property) is saved only after the email was sent; a failed send fails the job and is retried. Missing Gmail permission, a failing search, an unreadable email, the 50-conversation cap and corrupt state are each loud (alert email / warning), never silent.
**New permission:** manifest adds `gmail.readonly` (5 scopes now). Paste order matters on the live scheduler: Code.gs first, then appsscript.json, then run `setup` and click Allow (see the header of HubScheduler.gs). `paymentWatchPreview` shows what it finds in the last year without sending anything.
**Tests:** `apps-script/hub-scheduler/test/hub-scheduler.test.mjs` 334 checks (was 270): weekday schedule + validation, classification, extraction from the real Aug 18 shape (sanitised), start / dedupe / new / noise / weekend, 30- and 60-day asks, permission failure + retry, failed send, unreadable email, cap, corrupt state, setup preview, read-only enforcement.
**NOT done / next:** nothing marks an invoice paid yet. Once the first payment email that names an inspection invoice or WO arrives, build the matcher (read the fields, match to `Insp_Bookings` by WO / invoice number / amount, call `/insp/billing/mark-paid` with confirm for Brett to approve). Brett must paste the two files and run `setup` for it to start. A 30-day reminder in Claude is set separately for the day after he starts it.
