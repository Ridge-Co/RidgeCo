RIDGE CO — QUICKBOOKS EMAIL BACKFILL

Units and properties created in QuickBooks as sub-customers were saved without an email, so an invoice sent for a unit like "3014 N Calvert St Apt B" has nowhere to go. This copies each blank sub-customer's email down from its parent (a unit from its property, a property from its owner). Preview first — nothing is written until you tap Apply. Safe to re-run: anything that already has an email is left alone.

Already has an email — not changed

These sub-customers already carry an email, so the backfill leaves them alone. If one is wrong or stale (e.g. a property that should bill to its owner's address), tick it and tap Force the parent's email — that OVERWRITES the existing address with the one from its parent in QuickBooks (a property → its owner; a unit → its property). The "Will copy from" column names the exact source. Rows whose parent has no email can't be forced.

Skipped — no parent email to copy

These have no email anywhere above them in QuickBooks. That usually means the OWNER has no email on file in QuickBooks (even if the Hub shows one). Fix them in the ground-truth panel below: filter to the row, type the email, and Set — or set the email on the owner and re-run to cascade.
