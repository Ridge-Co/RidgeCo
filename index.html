WHO DO I NEED TO PAY
The owner pays the invoice, then the vendor gets paid. This reads both balances straight from QuickBooks and shows where each job sits — so a vendor waiting on money you've already collected is the top of the list rather than something you find out about when they call.

QUICKBOOKS MAPPING
Links each owner and vendor here to the customer or vendor you already have in QuickBooks. Anything left unlinked gets created in QuickBooks the first time you invoice it — which is how you end up with the same customer twice under slightly different names.
