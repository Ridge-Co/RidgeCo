Overview — Two Ways Forward

Thank you for the opportunity to look at the roof at 3101 Gibbons Ave. Based on our inspection of the failing area above the second-floor bathroom, this proposal lays out two separate options so you can choose the one that best fits your budget and timeline. They are independent — you select one.

Option A — Targeted Repair is the lower-cost path: we address the active problem area now. Because of the overall age and condition of the existing roof, this repair carries no guarantee or warranty, and we honestly recommend the full replacement instead.

Option B — Full Roof Replacement removes and replaces the entire roof system with new shingles and a fully warrantied installation. It costs more up front but resolves the underlying condition and is protected by a workmanship warranty plus the shingle manufacturer's warranty.

⚠ Ballpark, best-efforts inspection disclaimer This proposal is based on a visual inspection from accessible vantage points. Roof decking, framing, and areas beneath the existing shingles cannot be fully evaluated until work is underway. Hidden rot, prior improper repairs, or additional damaged decking are common on older roofs and may not be visible now. We make every effort to honor these numbers; should genuinely unforeseen conditions be uncovered, any additional work will be presented to you in writing as a change order for approval before we proceed (see Terms).

A full replacement of the roof covering with a new architectural asphalt-shingle system, installed to manufacturer specification so the material warranty is preserved. Scope of work:

Underlayment. New synthetic underlayment mechanically fastened across the full roof deck as a secondary moisture barrier.

Drip edge & starter. New metal drip edge along all eaves and rakes, and a sealed starter-strip course along the eaves for wind resistance.

Flashing. New/re-worked flashing at all roof penetrations, walls, and transitions (vent pipes, and any chimney or sidewall as applicable) to seal against leaks.
8
Ridge ventilation & ridge cap. Ridge/cap shingles installed at peaks and hips; balanced ridge ventilation maintained as required to keep the shingle manufacturer's warranty valid.
9
Cleanup & magnetic nail sweep. Full site cleanup with a magnetic sweep of the grounds for nails and debris; the property is left clean.
10
Permit & final check. Any required roofing permit pulled, and a final walk-through/inspection of the completed roof.

Terms & Conditions

1. Proposal validity

This proposal is valid for 30 days from the date above. Pricing assumes the two options are quoted together; selecting Option A does not obligate you to Option B or vice-versa.

2. Scope & hidden conditions / change orders

Prices cover the scope described above. If concealed conditions are discovered once work is underway (e.g., additional rotted decking beyond the included allowance, prior improper repairs, or structural framing damage), we will stop, document the condition, and present a written change order with pricing for your approval before continuing. You are never billed for extra work you have not approved in writing.

3. Additional decking

Replacement roof decking beyond the sheets included in your selected option is billed at $125 per 4'×8' sheet, installed, and only as actually needed and documented.

4. Estimate Integrity Clause

Pricing assumes a single, unified, mobilized project performed on a continuous timeline. If line items are selectively removed or descoped after acceptance, the remaining approved work is subject to a 15% price adjustment plus a $150 travel/mobilization fee to cover independent trip and setup overhead.

5. Materials & color selection

Shingle color and available product line are selected by the owner from current stock options prior to material ordering. Substitutions of equivalent-or-better materials may be made if a specified product is unavailable.

6. Access, staging & weather

Owner provides reasonable access to the property, driveway/curb space for a dumpster and materials, and access to electrical power. Roofing is weather-dependent; either job requires approximately four (4) consecutive dry days to perform. Start and completion dates are good-faith estimates and may shift for rain, high wind, or unsafe conditions. Weather delays are not a breach of this agreement.

7. Payment terms

Payments are due at the milestones shown for your selected option. Payment is by electronic methods only; physical checks are not accepted. Past-due balances may pause work until the account is current. Any first payment collected before work begins is applied to your contract price.

8. Warranty

Option A (Repair) is expressly sold AS-IS with no guarantee or warranty of any kind, given the end-of-life condition of the existing roof. Option B (Replacement) carries a 1-year Ridge Co workmanship warranty on the installation plus pass-through of the shingle manufacturer's limited warranty on materials. Workmanship warranty covers defects in installation, not damage from storms, impacts, other trades, foot traffic, or lack of maintenance.
