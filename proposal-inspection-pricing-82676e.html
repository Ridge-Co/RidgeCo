Overview

Ridge Co performs annual property inspections across your portfolio on a per-trip basis. Pricing is tiered by the number of units actually completed on a single visit — the more units inspected in one trip, the lower the per-unit rate. Inspections are conducted on a best-efforts basis, with photos and notes logged directly in AppFolio for your records.
