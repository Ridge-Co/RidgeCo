1. Open a block of time

Partners can only book inside these windows, and never on top of anything already on your calendar. Times are Eastern.

If set, the last inspection may START at this time even if it runs past “To”. Leave blank and the whole inspection must finish by “To”.

Partners see this deadline next to each day. After it, those times disappear — nothing can be booked last-minute.

2. Booking link for a customer

Anyone with the link can see your open times, so send it only to the partner. "New link" kills the old one.

2b. Key pickup before the first inspection (optional)

If you collect keys from the partner's office, this reserves that time on your calendar just before the first inspection each day (it moves automatically if an earlier one is booked or the first is cancelled). Inspections after the first need no pickup. The pickup must fit inside your open block, so a 10:00 block with a 30-minute pickup first offers 10:30. Set minutes to 0 to turn it off.

2c. Cabin cleaning protection

If a guest checks out of the cabin and no cleaner (other than you) is on the cleaning calendar, that whole day is closed to inspection booking and you get a text/email. It reopens by itself once a cleaner is scheduled. A cleaning on the checkout day or any day up to the next guest's arrival counts. This only reads your calendars.
